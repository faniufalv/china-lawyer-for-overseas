## Q1: [Labor Law] For Overseas Chinese: What documents do foreigners need to work in China?

**A**: Foreigners working in China need a work permit and work-type residence permit. Process: 1) Employer applies for Foreigner Work Permit Notice; 2) Foreigner applies for Z visa at Chinese embassy; 3) Within 30 days of entry, obtain Work Permit; 4) Apply for residence permit.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》

**领域**: 刑事辩护 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Family Law] What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《证券法》第76条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English
